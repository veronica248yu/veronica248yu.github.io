Wavelength

September 2025 - May 2026

Conflux Collective — Co-Project Lead  |  Cambridge, MA

Prototyping ~ Mechanical Design ~ Raspberry Pi ~ Electronics ~ User Testing

Problem Statement

Caption

What does it mean to be on the same wavelength? Wavelength is an interactive art installation where two participants must hum together for the sculpture to blossom.

Components:

  • a central telescoping tree (acts as the progress bar to completion)
  • a bed of kinetic flowers (provides immediate feedback on the harmonization between participants)
  • an ambient soundtrack that incorporates participants' voices

Tree top experimenting

Top view of cylinders

  • Calculated geometry constraints for relation between scissor lift and cylinders
  • Played around with tree top ideas: thought of using a lead screw to open an actual umbrella, but settled on a passive opening structure for weight reduction.
  • Integrated electronics to control motor speed with Raspberry Pi based on singing from two microphones.

Challenges

  • Initial print of 1 cylinder took 17 hours. Cut printing time down by 50% by using 45 deg rims.
  • Stabilized structure by including rails around each cylinder's rod.

Results

Test round

We can power the motor by being on the same wavelength. Mechanical parts ready to be connected to motor.
