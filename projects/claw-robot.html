Class Project  |  Cambridge, MA

Mechanical Design ~ SolidWorks ~ CNC Milling ~ Manual Lathing ~ Bandsaw & Drill Press

Problem Statement

Competition field

Robot challenges for a competition: pick up blocks, hockey pucks, and dog toys and place them in a tic-tac-toe board; traverse synthetic grass and 15° and 30° inclines.
